# 0022. v1 scope

## Decision

Full platform plus the management console (org/hierarchy/site/member/role/entitlement/audit administration) as the first real vertical slice.

## Why

Unlike a demo slice, every fork needs it and nobody deletes it; it exercises all three gates, ingest, storage, and audit end to end.

## Consequences

Largest v1; console UI is real product work.
